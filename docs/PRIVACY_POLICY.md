# Outrise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-08

Version: 2026-10-08

## 1. Who operates Outrise

Caleb Campbell operates Outrise in North Carolina, United States. Contact support@outrisefitness.com about privacy, access, corrections, or deletion. This notice describes the current beta. Hosting arrangements and provider log and backup retention remain under review before public deployment.

## 2. Information you provide

We store account email, username, a password hash rather than your original password, email-verification status, and account settings. Before account creation, a temporary registration request stores your email, chosen username, password hash, age-eligibility confirmation date, legal acknowledgment, and hashed verification state. Email ownership must be confirmed before an account or login session is created. Optional profile information includes names, name visibility, bio, and a profile image. We store faction membership, workout templates and sessions, exercise performance, personal records, XP, levels, achievements, competition contributions, verification submissions and review results, friendships, messages and read status, blocks, notifications, reports, suggestions, and moderation records. Legal acceptance stores a server date and document versions. We do not store your entered age or birth date; accepted and rejected age answers are recorded as dates. Registration can store a temporary age-retry deadline on your device.

## 3. Technical information and device storage

Requests and hosting services can expose IP addresses and connection information. The backend uses operational and error logs that may include account or socket identifiers and request details. Rate limits use request information to prevent abuse. The app stores session credentials and an age-retry deadline locally; native platforms use SecureStore and the web age-delay flow uses browser storage. Expo provides app development and distribution tooling. The app currently has no advertising or third-party marketing analytics integration.

## 4. How information is used

Information enables account access and recovery, workouts and progress calculations, factions and competition, messaging and notifications, uploads and verification review, support, moderation, abuse prevention, and application maintenance. Transactional emails include verification codes, password-reset codes, and support replies. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising in the current beta.

## 5. What other members see

Other members can see information exposed by profiles, leaderboards, and activity feeds, including usernames, profile images, roles, factions, achievements, approved performance information, and workout activity. Available visibility controls apply to names; they do not make every profile or activity private. Direct-message participants see the messages sent to them. Members can copy or screenshot information, and Outrise cannot remove those outside copies.

## 6. Staff access and reports

Authorized admins use support and moderation tools and review verification media. Reporting a message copies that specific message, its sender, timestamp, and your reporting reason into the admin inbox. It does not grant the inbox access to the entire conversation. The operator and authorized personnel with database or infrastructure access can technically access stored records, including messages, for support, security, maintenance, or legal needs. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Treat media links as shareable: a person who receives a working media URL may be able to view its contents. Owner-only tools show suggestions and privileged administration controls.

## 7. Service providers

MongoDB Atlas stores application records. Cloudinary stores and serves profile images and verification videos. Resend processes recipients and message content for transactional email. Cloudflare manages the domain and forwards support email to the operator’s destination mailbox; that mailbox provider also processes the correspondence. Expo supports app development and distribution. Render is the planned production backend host, not a provider assumed to be active before deployment. Providers process information necessary for their services and may retain their own security, operational, or delivery records. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to address security incidents or protect people’s rights.

## 8. Retention

Most account, workout, progress, relationship, and message records remain while the account exists unless removed through available features. Temporary registration requests and their password hashes expire after 10 minutes; requesting a new code renews that period. Expired requests are removed by database cleanup, which may run after the exact expiry time. A hashed email identifier enforces a registration-email cooldown and expires after 24 hours. Reviewed verification videos are eligible for removal 30 days after approval or rejection, while the result remains. Pending submissions expire without approval or rejection 30 days after submission. Their videos become eligible for removal, while the submission record remains and the user can submit again. Activity events are eligible for removal after 90 days, together with associated notification and dismissal state; owner announcement records have a separate lifecycle. Resolved support reports and closed suggestions are eligible for removal after 12 calendar months. Reports with pending email replies remain. Moderation history stays while an account is flagged, suspended, or banned; for active accounts, the cleanup records resolution when first observed and removes resolved history after 12 months. Old resolved records lacking dates receive a fresh timer. Reopening a suggestion clears its timer; saving it as closed starts a new timer. Scheduled cleanup depends on backend availability and retries failures. Separate provider logs and backup retention are not yet assigned a finalized duration, so this notice does not promise a fixed deadline for those copies.

## 9. Deletion and your choices

Edit supported profile fields, use name visibility, block users, dismiss activity or notifications, or initiate account deletion in the app. Deletion disables access and queues removal of associated live records and uploads rather than waiting for ordinary retention timers. Failures are retried, so completion is not instantaneous. Shared conversations remain usable for the other participant with the deleted account’s identity and its message content removed. Historical season winners remain. Backup copies and records independently held by providers or recipients can have a separate lifecycle. Contact support for access, correction, or other privacy requests; we may need to confirm your identity. Rights depend on applicable law, and this notice does not limit rights you have under that law.

## 10. Age eligibility

Outrise is for adults aged 18 or older and relies on self-declared age. A rejected existing-account answer persists until owner review; support is available for mistakes or when the person is now eligible. Before registration, a device stores a 24-hour retry delay after an under-18 answer. This is not independent age verification and can be bypassed on a different device. Contact support if you believe an underage person is using the service.

## 11. Security and changes

Passwords are hashed and verification or recovery codes are stored in protected hashed form. We use access controls and other safeguards, but cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not share passwords or sensitive information unnecessarily. If a security incident occurs, we will assess applicable notification duties. Updates receive a new document version, and the app presents the current documents for acknowledgment before continued access to the main features. Contact support@outrisefitness.com with questions.
